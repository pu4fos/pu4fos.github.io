Bibliography

Books worth owning, from first licence to deep experimentation. Most canonical references are in English; ISBNs are given where a stable edition could be confirmed.

Getting started

Ham Radio For Dummies

H. Ward Silver · ISBN 9781119454847

A friendly, thorough on-ramp: licensing, gear, bands and etiquette for the complete beginner.

Basic Radio: Understanding the Key Building Blocks

Joel Hallas, W1ZR · ISBN 9780872599550

Explains the fundamental blocks of a radio — receivers, transmitters, antennas — in plain language.

The ARRL Ham Radio License Manual

ARRL Inc.

The standard study guide for the entry-level licence exam (updated per exam cycle); pairs with local regulations.

Antennas & propagation

The ARRL Antenna Book for Radio Communications (25th ed.)

ARRL Inc. · ISBN 9781625951762

The deepest single reference on amateur antennas, transmission lines and propagation. The one antenna book to own.

Antennas for All Applications

John D. Kraus & Ronald Marhefka

A rigorous university-level classic on antenna theory — for readers who want the mathematics behind the practice.

Electronics & homebrew

Experimental Methods in RF Design

Hayward, Campbell & Larkin · ISBN 9780872599239

A beloved bench-oriented guide to designing and building your own RF circuits and small transceivers.

Understanding Basic Electronics (2nd ed.)

Walter Banzhaf, WB1ANE · ISBN 9780872590823

A step-by-step introduction to electricity, components and simple circuits — the groundwork for homebrewing.

The Art of Electronics (3rd ed.)

Paul Horowitz & Winfield Hill · ISBN 9780521809269

Not ham-specific, but the definitive practical electronics text; invaluable once you start designing circuits.

Reference & operating

The ARRL Handbook for Radio Communications

ARRL Inc.

The annual, encyclopedic reference covering theory, circuits, antennas and construction. Updated every year.

The ARRL Operating Manual for Radio Amateurs

ARRL Inc. · ISBN 9781625950598

How to actually operate: DXing, contesting, digital modes, awards, nets and emergency communications.

Two-Way Radios and Scanners For Dummies

H. Ward Silver · ISBN 9780764595820

A broad, accessible look at two-way radio beyond the ham bands — useful context for newcomers.

Regional societies & study material

LABRE — study material & Brazilian regulations

LABRE / Anatel

For operators in Brazil: LABRE's study material and Anatel's rules define the local exam and callsign classes (PY/PU).

URE / national society manuals

IARU member societies

Spanish-speaking readers should also consult their national IARU society (e.g., URE in Spain) for local study guides and band plans.
