Contact

Thanks for wanting to get in touch. PU4FOS is a one-person amateur-radio project, so replies are personal and may take a little time.

On the air

Operating mostly VHF/UHF and satellites from grid GH80ae, Belo Horizonte, Brazil. Listen for the call PU4FOS.

QSL & logs

QSOs are confirmed via QRZ.com and ARRL Logbook of The World (LoTW). See my full profile and preferred QSL route on QRZ.

Feedback on the tools

Found a bug, a wrong translation, or have an idea for a new calculator? That feedback is very welcome — it is what keeps this site useful. The best route is a message through my QRZ page.

This site does not collect messages through a form; please use the channels above.
