Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PU4FOS website (pu4fos.github.io) collects, uses and protects information when you visit. The site is a personal, non-commercial amateur-radio project. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Who we are

The site is operated by an individual amateur-radio operator (callsign PU4FOS), based in Belo Horizonte, Brazil. You can reach us through the contact page.

What data we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect personal information you type. The tools on this site (log signing, mapping, locators) run entirely in your browser and do not upload your files to us. We do collect standard, anonymous usage data through the third-party services below.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies for two purposes: to measure traffic (analytics) and to show advertising. Some cookies are set only after you give consent for personalization.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used (pages viewed, approximate region, device type). This helps us improve the content. Analytics runs in a privacy-conscious mode with IP handling restricted. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense and advertising

This site displays ads via Google AdSense to help cover hosting and time. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads. Non-personalized ads are shown even without your consent; if you accept, ads and content may be personalized. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can learn more and opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

Your consent choices

When required, a consent banner lets you accept or reject personalized advertising. In regions where a certified Consent Management Platform is mandatory, we rely on Google's own CMP. You can change your choice at any time with the button, also available in the footer of every page.

Data retention and your rights

Aggregated analytics data is retained according to Google's defaults. Depending on your location (for example under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not maintain user accounts, most requests are handled by adjusting your consent choices or by contacting the relevant third party directly.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
